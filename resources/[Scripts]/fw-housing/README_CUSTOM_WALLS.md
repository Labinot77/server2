# Custom walls (fw-custom-walls)

40 wall panels: 8 materials (plaster, light / dark hardwood, tile, grey / red brick, stone, white marble) x 5 sizes
(XS 0.5x0.5, Small 1x1, Medium 1x2.5, Large 2x2.5, XL 3x3 m, 10 cm thick). Each has collision and 16 colours.
Overlap them in the editor to build any shape.

## Build
1. Blender + Sollumz (with PyMateria for binary files). Save an empty .blend in a new folder.
2. Scripting > Open `tools/make_custom_walls.py` > Run Script. Textures are generated by the script.
3. Copy `<folder>/fw-custom-walls` to your server, add `ensure fw-custom-walls`, restart, clear your FiveM cache.

## In game
Decorate > Structure (grid icon) > the panels with a paint bucket. Right click a placed panel > Colour, or press Y.
Materials, sizes and colours live in `Config.CustomWalls` (config/sh_mlo.lua) and at the top of the script:
change both together and rebuild.
